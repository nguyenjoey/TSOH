Capability dependency map

Twelve components regrouped from Karl Garcia’s “Beyond Dependence” article. Each row depends on the columns marked. Select a component or a mark to see how the dependency works.

Key readings

  • Load-bearing: money and land rules support seven other components, verification six, industrial clusters six, and learning five.
  • One hard loop: clusters need trained workers, and training needs anchor employers. Every other hard dependency runs one way, from rules to backbone to buyers to payoffs.
  • Critical path: political continuity → verification → money and land rules → power and water → industrial clusters → learning → shared gains.
  • Maritime repair, research, shared gains and resilience have no hard dependents, so each needs an explicit owner and measure.

Of the 51 dependencies, 27 are hard (the dependent can’t work at scale without the supplier) and 24 are soft. The links and their labels are one analyst’s judgment from public evidence, not measured data. Draft, 10 November 2026.
